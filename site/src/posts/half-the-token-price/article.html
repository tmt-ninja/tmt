We ran our Codex members on Terra for a day and a few night shifts, then went back to Sol. It felt like Terra burned more. Here is what our logs show.

The sample

Sol did 120 PRs at medium or high effort. Terra did 18, mostly at high. High vs high is the fair comparison: 10 Terra PRs and 42 Sol PRs.

THE PATTERN

Terra reads about three times as much for each line it writes. At half the price that lands on the same dollars, and it needed more fixes before merge.

It is not the effort setting

Raising Sol from medium to high did not make it read more per line. Terra read more at every setting, including medium.
