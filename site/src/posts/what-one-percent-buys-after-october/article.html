Claude Ultra in November: $32/1% on Opus, $21–24/1% once Fable enters

In October, Opus-heavy Ultra weeks gave $30–35 per 1% while Sonnet-heavy weeks gave $26–29. November's local transcripts show a sharper version of the same split after two leads began running Fable 5.2 on 11 November:

Opus 5.5Fable 5.2Sonnet 5.5

  • Opus-only weeks held $32–37 per 1%—slightly above October's $26–35 range once Sonnet workers retired.
  • Local $/1% fell to $24 (C3) and $20.5 (C4) as Fable 5.2 grew to 24% and 33% of spend. That does not prove Ultra's allowance shrank: Fable 5.2 charges 2× Opus on input/output/cache-writes, yet keeps cache reads at $0.20/MTok (same as Opus). Because our agents are mostly cache reads (1.47B in C3, 1.40B in C4), any higher meter weight on Fable lowers measured API $/1%—and off-Mac claude.ai use counts on the meter but not in local transcripts.
  • Runway: with workers moved to Codex on 15 November, Ultra covered 6–7 leads + PM for a full 7-day week (C3 ended with 6% left).

Codex Pro 200 → Pro Lite (10x): why model mix changed the headline

Direct rollout-file audits across November's three Codex windows—including the 18 November downgrade from Pro 200 (20x) to Pro Lite (10x)—reveal two things raw quota % hides:

Sol 6.1Terra 6.1Astra 6Sol 6.2Astra 6.2

  • Pro 200 did not double in API dollars after 29 October. W1 (75% Terra) landed at $7.40/1% ($740/wk) and W2 (48% Terra) at $9.08/1% ($908/wk)—consistent with our earlier finding that Terra moves the meter faster per API dollar than Sol.
  • Pro Lite (10x) held ~$656—72–89% of Pro 200's November value at half the price. When our downgrade took effect overnight on 18 November, the meter jumped 86% → 100% with no overnight work. Yet the first full 10x window (W3, 98% used) still carried $643 of Standard-rate compute ($6.56/1%).
  • Why W3 emptied in ~2 active working days: one seat running gpt-6.2-astra (4× Sol's price) ate $352—55% of the entire week. Without Astra, 4–5 Sol workers would stretch Pro Lite roughly 4 active days; with Pro 200, the same team ran 5–7 days.

Google AI Pro and practical team sizing

  • Google AI Pro (1 seat, Gemini 4 Hydrogen): on 17 November, core-agy used 256M input (244M cached) and 0.98M output tokens across 47 quota points—$66–121 by our PM's estimate (cached and output prices assumed), or $1.4–2.6 per 1% (~$140–257/week). That covered ~2.5 busy days for one specialist worker.
  • What our team actually needs per week: 6–7 reviewing leads on Claude Ultra ($200) + 4–6 coding workers on ChatGPT Pro 200 ($200) clears a 7-day week cleanly. Cutting Codex to Pro Lite ($100) works only if we keep gpt-6.2-astra off routine seats; adding Google AI Pro buys ~2.5 extra days for one overflow worker.
  • Hygiene still beats plan tier: adding our wait guard on 3 November cut Codex sleep/poll calls from 17–26% down to 3–9%, and bundling tickets cut reviews per finished ticket by a fifth.
TAKEAWAY

Comparing subscription tiers without splitting models misleads on both providers: measured local $/1% on Ultra fell by a third once Fable 5.2 entered (where cache reads stay $0.20), while one Astra 6.2 seat ate 55% of our Pro Lite week. Watch model mix first, plan tier second.

How we measured

  • SOURCESLocal Claude Code transcripts (~/.claude/projects, deduped by message + request ID) and Codex rollout files (~/.codex/sessions, per-file token deltas attributed to turn_context model) on the one Mac the team runs on.
  • PRICESPublic Standard short-context API list prices: Opus 5.5 $4/$5/$8/$0.20/$20, Sonnet 5.5 $2/$2.5/$4/$0.10/$10, Fable 5.2 $8/$10/$16/$0.20/$40 (input / 5m write / 1h write / read / output per MTok); Sol 6.1 & 6.2 $2/$0.10/$10, Terra 6.1 $1/$0.05/$5, Astra 6 $10/$1.00/$50, Astra 6.2 $8/$0.40/$40. OpenAI's >272k long-context multiplier is not applied, so W3 is a lower bound.
  • EXCLUSIONS & GAPScodex-auto-review does not count against our meter and is excluded. 6 November was an all-rest day (no rollout folder on disk). Off-Mac usage (e.g. claude.ai web) counts on the meter but not in local files, making Claude $/1% a lower bound. C1's 86% is a lower bound (last reading 29h before reset); C4 is a partial week (59%).
  • GOOGLE AI PROSingle-seat 17 November measurement (47 quota points); the $140–257/week spread reflects cached/output pricing assumptions, and the subscription price is pending owner confirmation.
